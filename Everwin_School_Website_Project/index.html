A Legacy of Inspiring Brilliance & Character

Founded with a vision to make high-quality, holistic education accessible, Everwin Group of Schools has grown from a humble beginning into one of Chennai's most trusted educational institutions.

With over 25,000 students and a dedicated faculty team, we combine rigorous academic frameworks (CBSE & State Board) with modern technological advancements and strong moral values.

Help & Info

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about admissions, curricula, and campus life.

What boards of education are offered at Everwin Group of Schools?
Everwin offers both CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education) and Tamil Nadu State Board (Matriculation) curricula across our different campus branches in Chennai.
How can I schedule a campus visit or counseling session?
You can fill out our online admission enquiry form on this website or call our centralized admission helpline at +91 44 2651 2222 to schedule a guided campus tour.
Does Everwin provide safe school bus transportation?
Yes, Everwin operates a comprehensive fleet of modern GPS-tracked school buses covering key pickup routes across Chennai with dedicated female attendants on board.
What co-curricular and sports facilities are available?
We offer professional coaching in cricket, football, basketball, skating, athletics, martial arts, classical dance, Western music, chess, and robotics.
